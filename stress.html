⚡ Stress test boards × layers × text

Each preset opens the app and fills the document immediately: 2048×2048 boards in a grid, each layer painted with colored diagonal stripes (all layers remain visible). One full layer = 64 chunks = 16 MB of pixels. Text items are real layers, interleaved into each board stack.

4 × 4 full256 MB Warm-up: smooth everywhere, including zoom-out. 8 × 8 full1 GB Comfort zone: smooth drawing, zoom-out still manageable. 16 × 8 full2 GB Recommended limit: at zoom-out, draw calls and uploads become noticeable. 16 × 16 at 40%~1.6 GB Realistic: partial chunk-level coverage, like a real document. 16 × 14 full + 2 text layers~3.5 GB Two vector text layers interleaved into EVERY board stack: sandwich stacks, the worst case for planes and zoom-out proxies — text is baked into quads. 16 × 16 full4 GB ⚠ The wall: the wasm32 heap ceiling — filling stops by itself and the panel reports where. “Fit All” may lose the WebGL context.
In the app: use the ⚡ toolbar button for presets, custom values (boards, layers, coverage, text layers per board), and live stats (fps, peak frame, chunks, CPU/heap/GPU). After filling: zoom out, pan between boards, draw on a layer, and watch fps and peak frame time. URL: ?stress=BxL[xCOV%][tN] — e.g. ?stress=8x8x50t3 = 8 boards × 8 layers at 50% with 3 text layers per board (t alone = 1). Pure JS engine: add &engine=js.
